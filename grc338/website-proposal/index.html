Final project proposal

Introduction

Cosmic Cafe

A local, family owned cafe that specializes in coffee, but offers other food items, namely brunch and lunch items.

Target audience

The primary users of the site will be people local to SLO and Cal Poly students.

The webstie will be used to view the menu, place orders ahead for pickup, learn more about company, and shop for coffee grounds and merch. We want to convey the values of the website to our customers, and show what our shop is all about.

Home

We are a family owned coffee shop with a simple goal – to give our customers the best coffee, food, and service that we possibly can. Our eccentric decor comes from my various art and memorabilia collections. I wanted the shops’ personalities to shine through as a local 80s and 90s pop-culture mecca. We aren’t coffee snobs, we aren’t a corporate chain, just a simple place you can come and relax. We invite you to come try it for yourself!
