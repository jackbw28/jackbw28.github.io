About us

We are a family owned coffee shop with a simple goal – to give our customers the best coffee, food, and service that we possibly can. We wanted to create a welcoming, calming environment where anyone can come in and feel like they were right at home. We aren’t coffee snobs, we aren’t a corporate chain, just a simple place you can come and relax. We invite you to come try it for yourself!
